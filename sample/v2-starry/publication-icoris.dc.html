Comparative Analysis of Fungal Infections Classification in Apple Leaves

Carlo, H. N., Andreas, K., & Zakiyyah, A. Y.

VENUE
6th ICORIS
YEAR
2024
TOPIC
CNN + GLCM texture analysis

Abstract

This paper co-authors a comparative study of standard convolutional neural networks against CNNs augmented with texture analysis via the Gray-Level Co-occurrence Matrix (GLCM), applied to classifying fungal infections in apple leaves. The analysis focuses on feature-extraction efficiency, examining where texture-aware features help a CNN separate visually similar disease classes and where they add cost without a meaningful accuracy gain.

Why it matters

Reliable, lightweight plant-disease classifiers are a practical building block for on-device agricultural tools — the same category of CNN feature-extraction trade-offs studied here recur in mobile deployment work like the CoreML pipelines built for Buah Hati.
